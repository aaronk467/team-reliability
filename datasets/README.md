# Demonstration data

These fictional dispatch logs are generated with Python's standard library and seed 467. They contain no patient information and support no clinical performance claims. From the repository root, run `python datasets/generate.py` to regenerate them. To compare regenerated bytes without changing the tracked files, use `python datasets/generate.py --output-dir /tmp/responsewatch-datasets`.

| File | Rows | Scenario |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `stable.csv` | 1,781 | A time-stable generator with right-skewed durations, varying demand, case mix, and occasional long delays. It can still produce chart signals and miss targets. |
| `south_shift.csv` | 1,781 | Same underlying calls, with five minutes added to South responses from January 21 onward. |
| `improvement.csv` | 1,781 | Same underlying calls, with response duration multiplied by 0.72 across all zones from January 21 onward. |

Each file spans January 1–February 14, 2026. January 1–20 is the default reference; January 21–February 14 is monitoring. Calls are paired across scenarios, so these are three controlled demonstrations rather than independent samples. There are 845 high-priority and 936 standard-priority calls; zone totals are Central 554, South 428, North 403, and East 396. Daily zone counts range from 2 to 18 rather than being fixed. There are 1,204 day-shift and 577 night-shift calls. Arrivals can occur on the following date; grouping uses UTC dispatch time.

Daily demand is Poisson, with zone means North 9, Central 12, South 10, and East 8, multiplied by 1.08 on weekdays or 0.85 on weekends. Day-shift dispatch probability is 0.70 on weekdays and 0.62 on weekends; high-priority probability is 0.50 by day and 0.44 by night. Traffic and units-available categories have different day/night distributions. Response durations combine a lognormal multiplier (log-scale standard deviation 0.22), priority-specific centers, zone offsets, measured-context multipliers, and a 3.5% chance of an additional 7–14-minute delay. The generator source provides the exact parameters.

These correlations are planted illustrative mechanisms, not estimates from an ambulance service. Context is measured at dispatch; delay-reason annotations are partial and may be assigned after the outcome. In `stable`, 596/1,781 calls have a recorded reason; blank entries mean unknown/unrecorded, not no delay. South deterioration annotations are deliberately assigned `unit_unavailable` after the change; this is a simulation label and does not validate causal inference from field annotations.

The eight-minute default is a demonstration threshold. Analyze comparable priorities before comparing zones.
